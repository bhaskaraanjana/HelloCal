# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Busy individuals tracking nutrition/calories daily. Their context is rapid on-the-go tracking, relying on speed, intelligent voice inputs, and reminders to log their food and supplements before they forget.

## Product Purpose
HelloCal is an intelligent voice calorie tracker that simplifies tracking meals and supplements. It aims to reduce logging friction through smart voice processing, timely granular reminders, and beautiful interactive feedback.

## Brand Personality
Obsidian aura (mysterious, sleek, and premium dark).
- 3-word personality: Sleek, Precise, Intelligent.
- Emotional goals: Confidence, speed, calm focus.

## Anti-references
- Saturated AI-cream/beige/sand SaaS templates (the standard warm-neutral SaaS style).
- Bland, plain gray/dark grid layouts with no lighting or tactical visual hierarchy.
- Over-the-top, distracting cyberpunk aesthetics that hurt readability and usability.
- Bright, clinical, hospital-like white spreadsheets.

## Design Principles
- **Frictionless Entry**: Place voice and quick log flows at the focal point of interaction; speed beats decoration.
- **Atmospheric Depth**: Use obsidian dark surfaces, layered glassmorphism, and neon glows to guide attention and group information without visual clutter.
- **Intelligent Feedback**: Animate responses and states with purpose, avoiding generic transitions or hover animations on static elements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strictly maintain WCAG AA contrast (especially for light/muted text on dark glass backgrounds).
- Honor system-level `prefers-reduced-motion` settings.
- Ensure large touch targets on mobile (≥ 44px) for rapid on-the-go logging.
